L858D Language Switcher
Release version 0.4.0

Purpose
Switch a compatible Sekonic L-858D / L-858D-U to English, Japanese or Simplified Chinese.
The app selects languages already included in the original firmware. It does not flash
firmware or change the meter's region or radio configuration. Its English/Chinese app
interface is independent of the meter language. This is not an official Sekonic product.

Installation and requirements
• macOS 13 or later. One universal app supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
• A data-capable Micro-B USB cable; connect only one powered-on meter.
• Firmware exactly 17, 18 or 19; reported region 0000, 0001, 0002 or 0003.
• Current meter language English, Japanese or Chinese.
Extract the Mac app ZIP, move L858D Language Switcher.app to Applications and open it.
The app is self-contained. Switching supported firmware needs no Python, Homebrew, REA,
Xcode, Sekonic Data Transfer Software (DTS), additional drivers or firmware files.
The separate source ZIP is for developers. A Windows app is not provided.

How to use
1. Quit older Language Switchers, Sekonic DTS, its firmware updater and other meter tools.
2. Connect one meter with a data cable, power it on and click the detection button.
3. Check the model, firmware, region, current language and validation notice.
4. Choose the target meter language and save. Keep USB and power connected until finished.
5. After the save acknowledgment, unplug USB, power off and on, and check the menu language.
   You can reconnect and detect again to verify the result.
The current-language button is disabled, so an unchanged language is not saved again.
To restore Japanese or English, use the same process with that language. Changing the
app interface language does not change the meter.

Other firmware versions
Only the explicit versions 17, 18 and 19 are allowed. Unsupported versions stop with no
bypass. For older firmware, use official Sekonic DTS to upgrade to 19 first:
1. Quit this utility. Select L-858D on the official download page, obtain DTS for a
   compatible computer, and read Sekonic's software guide.
2. Use DTS's Update Setting tab and follow the official update instructions for 19.
   Keep power and USB connected during the update; unplug and restart when instructed.
3. Fully quit DTS and its updater, reconnect the meter, open this utility and detect it.
   Switch language after firmware 19 is identified.
There is no need to upgrade 17, 18 or 19 just to switch language. This utility contains
no updater, downgrader or Sekonic firmware. Research downgrades are not installation steps.
DTS has separate system requirements; this utility's macOS 13+ support does not establish
DTS compatibility with those systems. If only a newer unsupported firmware is offered,
or the meter is already newer than 19, retain the result, contact Sekonic support and wait;
do not downgrade on your own.
Official software: https://sekonic.com/downloads/
Japanese support: https://www.sekonic.co.jp/product/support/

Physical validation
All physical tests used one Japanese-region (0002) unit.
• 17 and 19: Chinese switching, saving, readback and persistence after unplugging and
  restarting were verified.
• 18: English setter/save/readback and a later independent read passed. Existing Chinese
  survived the firmware transition and a confirmed restart. A fresh Chinese/Japanese
  write was not tested on 18; its English restart steps were not individually confirmed.
• 17: retained English from 18 survived a confirmed restart and readback; this is not a
  fresh English setter test on 17.
• 19: Japanese and English save/readback passed, but their separate restart persistence
  was not confirmed.
Regions 0000 (U.S. L-858D-U), 0001 (another reviewed region) and 0003 (Chinese default)
have static firmware and offline-test evidence only. All three language operations have
protocol evidence, but not every language direction, region or hardware batch was tested.
See Compatibility and Verification.txt for the detailed limits.

Troubleshooting
• Not found: check power, data cable and connection; try a direct Mac connection.
• Cannot obtain exclusive access: quit other meter software and detect again.
• Unsupported region/language: keep the operation log; checks cannot be bypassed.
• Interrupted or uncertain operation: do not repeatedly press language buttons. State
  may have changed. After the app stops, unplug, power off/on, check the menu, then reconnect.
• Hide the serial number before sharing logs. Logs are stored locally.

Opening on another Mac
The app is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. macOS may be unable to verify its developer.
After checking the source and file integrity, try opening it, then use Open Anyway in
System Settings > Privacy & Security if offered. Stop and investigate an explicit malware
or damaged-file warning. Do not disable system security or remove download security attributes.
Apple guidance: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

Saving and resetting
The original settings-save function persists the current SETT block, including language.
This is not a full-device backup or calibration-recovery tool; measurement accuracy was
not calibrated. A factory reset may restore the region's default language.
